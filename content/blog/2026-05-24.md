Finally an update!

Most of the footage was recorded shortly after the previous video (29/03/2026), but I simply didn’t have time the to edit it until now. April and May were very busy months, with a lot of film projects and school deadlines. I helped a lot of schoolmates on their filmsets for their final projects, and was also busy making my second big shortfilm (stay tuned!!)

After just 2 days of practicing the 'City Of Lights' track in the 'Paris Drone Festival' map, I already hit my goal!

That meant I could treat myself, and buy the BETAFPV LiteRadio 4. So far, I'm really enjoying the controller. It's small, so easy to take with me in my backpack, and it uses USB-C, so I don't have to carry around some weird cable all the time.

I also bought the 'Liftoff: Micro Drones' simulator, to practice flying with tinywhoops. The physics for the Air75 should be pretty realistic. I did change the default 1610 2-blade props for 1622 3-blade props, to match the Air75 II better.

Flying inside and through small gaps is going better and better, so I'm really excited to buy my first drone!
